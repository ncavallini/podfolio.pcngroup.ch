Do you make a podcast?

Add a “Listen on PodFolio” button to your website or newsletter. Whoever clicks it listens to your podcast in the browser, without installing anything.

  1. Paste your podcast's RSS feed address.
  2. Copy the link, or the HTML code for the button.
  3. Put it wherever you like: website, newsletter, bio, episode notes.

You find the RSS feed in your hosting platform's dashboard (Spreaker, Podbean, Spotify for Creators, Libsyn…).

Questions

Really no ads?

PodFolio shows no ads at all. Some hosts read ads inside their own episodes, though: those are part of the audio and stay, as in any podcast app.

How much does it cost?

For 14 days you can use everything for free, no card needed. Then you unlock PodFolio Premium with a single CHF 3.99 purchase (or the equivalent in your currency): no subscription, no renewal. Until you buy it the app stays locked, but your library and downloads stay on the device.

Which devices does it work on?

On Android, from Google Play, and in any modern browser at app.podfolio.pcngroup.ch, so it works on iPhone, iPad and computers too. Sign in with Google and your subscriptions, progress and queue follow you everywhere.

Do I pay again for the web version?

No. Premium is bought once in the Android app and belongs to your Google account: sign in with the same account in the web version and it's already unlocked.

Can I bring my podcasts from my current app?

Yes: export your subscriptions as an OPML file from your current app and import it in PodFolio's settings. You can also paste a Spotify show link into search. And you can export your subscriptions whenever you like.

What data do you collect?

Only what the app needs to work, and we don't sell it. The details are in the privacy policy.
