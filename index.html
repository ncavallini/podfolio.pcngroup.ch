Support for the Il Post subscriber feed

PodFolio includes a dedicated flow for Il Post subscribers, with sign-in support and manual podcast import using the slug from the Il Post URL.

Il Post

Designed for subscriber-only podcast access

In Settings, users can sign in with their abbonati.ilpost.it credentials, unlock their Il Post access, and add supported podcasts directly to the PodFolio library.

Il Post podcasts become part of the regular library, so new episodes, playback, sync, and downloads work just like the rest of PodFolio.

How it works

A simple import flow

  1. 01 Sign in with the same credentials you use on abbonati.ilpost.it.
  2. 02 Paste the final part of the podcast URL, for example the slug after ilpost.it/podcasts/.
  3. 03 Add the show to your library and keep listening with the same player, sync, and download experience.
