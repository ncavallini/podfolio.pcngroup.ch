    Questions

    PlatformsWhere can I use PodFolio?

    On Android, from Google Play, and in any modern browser at podfolio-10b3f.web.app. On an iPhone, an iPad or a computer, use the web app.

    Web appDo I pay again for the web app?

    No. Premium is bought once in the Android app and belongs to your Google account. Sign in with the same account in the web app and it is unlocked there too.

    Il PostWhat happens to my Il Post password?

    It is used only to sign in to Il Post. In the web app the request passes through a PCN Group proxy on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards it without storing or logging it. Il Post shows are never turned into share links.

    SwitchingCan I bring my podcasts with me?

    Yes. Import an OPML file from Settings, or paste a Spotify show link into search. You can export your subscriptions as OPML whenever you like.

    LanguageWhich language is the app in?

    Italian. Search finds podcasts in every language, and they play as their makers published them.

    After the trialWhat happens after 14 days?

    PodFolio asks for the one-time Premium unlock, CHF 3.99. Until you buy it the app stays locked, and your library and downloads stay on the device.
